    03 / Behind the answers

    A rulebook the agent can use.

    The Open Knowledge Format (OKF) bundle stores the business concepts and policies. The governed agent consults it as it works.

    1

    Understand the question

    Resolve terms such as “net bookings.” Apply an agreed default or ask for clarification when a term is ambiguous.

    2

    Use the approved rules

    Choose the right metric, fiscal period, and currency. Check disclosure restrictions before producing an answer.

    3

    Show what supports it

    Return the result with its source and calculation receipt. Flag outdated definitions or decline unsupported metrics.

    A few useful terms, explained
    Semantic layer
    A shared set of business definitions, calculation rules, and policies that sits between a question and the data.
    OKF bundle
    The project's collection of machine-readable knowledge records, including metrics, dimensions, and policies.
    Governed agent
    The AI agent instructed to consult the semantic layer. It still has SQL access, so the evaluation checks whether it follows the rules.
    Net bookings
    The signed value of booking movements, including negative cancellations and changes. This project uses an approved fixed USD basis.
    Fiscal year (FY)
    Averio's business year runs October–September and is named for its ending year. FY2025 starts on 1 October 2024.
    Calculation receipt
    A record linking a result to the approved computation that produced it. Attestation checks that computation; it does not certify the entire AI answer.
    Passed / incorrect
    Whether an answer passed this experiment's automated scoring checks. A pass is not a guarantee that every statement is correct.
    Model confidence
    The model's own 0–100 estimate of its answer, stated at the end of every reply. Low shows tan, medium amber, high mint. It is self-reported — compare it with the automated check: a highly confident incorrect answer is exactly what the semantic layer exists to prevent.
    Layer confidence
    Shown only on governed answers, and calculated rather than self-reported: a 0–100 score from a fixed points rubric over the layer's own signals — attestation receipts (40), human- vs machine-verified definitions (25/10), freshness (25), and dataset corroboration (10). Hover the chip to see exactly how the points were earned. “Policy-based” means the reply served no computed figure (a refusal or clarification) — there is nothing to score, which describes the kind of evidence, not a defect.
    Abstention
    Saying a request cannot be answered under the available business rules, instead of inventing an unsupported definition.
    Caveat
    A required qualification the layer attaches to certain figures so they are not misread. Example: positive booking movements may be reported, but only with the warning that they include corrections and reclassifications and are not an approved “gross new bookings” metric. When a question calls for such a figure, the scoring checks that the caveat was actually stated (“caveat attached”) — a right number served without its caveat still counts as incorrect.
